# Code of conduct

## The short version

This project is a professional, welcoming space. Treat everyone with
respect. Assume good intent. Disagree about code, not about people.

## Expected behaviour

- Be considerate, constructive, and patient in issues, pull requests, and
  reviews.
- Give and accept feedback graciously. Critique the work, not the person.
- Respect that contributors bring different backgrounds and levels of
  experience.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory comments of any kind.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing another person's private information without consent.
- Sustained disruption of discussion.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces: the repository, issues, pull requests,
and any communication carried out on behalf of the project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to info@kaelumtechnologies.com. Reports are
handled confidentially. The maintainers may remove comments, reject
contributions, or block contributors who breach this code.
